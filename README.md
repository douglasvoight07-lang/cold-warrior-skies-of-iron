# Cold Warrior: Skies of Iron

An arcade-style military flight simulator set during the Cold War era. The target feel is closer to **Ace Combat** than DCS World — historically grounded, HOTAS-first, and built around a persistent campaign map.

## Design Pillars

- **Accessible realism** — Aircraft feel distinct from one another (a Phantom handles differently than a Crusader) but margins for error are generous. Players feel competent.
- **Historical atmosphere** — Missions and aircraft are grounded in real conflicts. Opening era: U.S. Navy operations from Yankee Station, Gulf of Tonkin, Vietnam War (1965–1973).
- **HOTAS-first controls** — All primary actions map to stick and throttle. A raw axis/button input layer is always exposed so players can rebind everything.
- **Sandbox with campaign spine** — Players fly freely over a persistent theater map. Campaign missions unlock organically through sorties, enemy attrition, and story beats.
