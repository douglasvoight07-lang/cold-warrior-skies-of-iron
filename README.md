# Cold Warrior: Skies of Iron

A Cold War-era aerial combat game.

## Overview

Cold Warrior: Skies of Iron puts players in the cockpit during the tense decades of superpower rivalry. Fly missions across contested airspace, manage fuel and ordnance, and survive engagements where one mistake ends the mission.
